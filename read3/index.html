Gmail Classroom Lab

Two-page Firebase demonstration

Choose your page

Students use the student page to connect their own Gmail and deliberately share one email. Instructors use the administrator page to review only the messages that students chose to share.

Privacy: The student must sign in, approve Google's read-only Gmail permission, retrieve a message, review it, and press Share with Instructor. No automatic inbox transfer or hidden background monitoring is implemented.
Educational prototype • Firebase-backed • Consent-based
