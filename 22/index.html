Meet Ashezi

Our founder’s vision

At nine, Ashezi saw that children in the camps around her had lost their homes, their books and their classrooms.

She began gathering them under trees and in temporary shelters to read together. That small circle has grown into Book Buddies, restoring learning for vulnerable children across Nigeria.

What we do

Six ways we put books in small hands

📚

Literacy Clubs in IDP Camps

Safe, structured reading circles giving displaced children in Abuja and Maiduguri books and guided learning.

👧

Girl-Child Education Support

Books, mentorship and safe learning spaces so girls in underserved communities can stay in school.

💻

Digital Literacy Hubs

Introducing children in underserved communities to digital skills and technology.

🍎

Teacher Support Initiative

Resources, training and sustained support for educators in fragile learning environments.

🌱

Youth Ambassador Programme

Empowering young leaders aged 11–24 to champion literacy and education equity.

🎪

Community & Outreach

Literacy festivals, storytelling sessions and events that celebrate reading.
