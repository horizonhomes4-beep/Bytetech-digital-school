Who we are

Every child deserves the chance to read, learn and dream

A youth-led non-profit transforming lives through literacy, education, leadership and advocacy, founded in October 2023 by then nine-year-old author Ashezi Akwashiki.

Today, Book Buddies empowers children in underserved communities, rural schools and IDP camps across Nigeria with safe spaces where reading builds confidence and hope.

Our work includes reading clubs, book drives, literacy festivals, teacher training, community libraries, mentorship, digital learning and the Book Buddies Changemakers Club.

We have reached over 5,000 children, distributed more than 10,000 books and raised over ₦14 million for literacy programmes.

🎯

Our mission

To empower children and young people through literacy, education, leadership, digital inclusion and community-driven initiatives that unlock their potential and inspire lasting social change.

🌍

Our vision

A world where every child has equitable access to books, quality education and the chance to become a lifelong learner and transformational leader.
