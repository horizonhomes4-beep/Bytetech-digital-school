Reading fluency workspace

Read aloud. Get feedback. Practise again.

The reading workspace includes published passages, microphone-based speech recognition, word tracking, elapsed time, estimated words per minute, and an end-of-session report. The floating Coach Reed panel stays beside the workspace and uses the browser's speech voice with animated mouth movement.

Recognition note: speech recognition depends on the browser, internet connection, microphone, accent, and background noise. It cannot guarantee perfect pronunciation scoring. Use a current Chrome or Edge browser over HTTPS for the strongest supported experience.
