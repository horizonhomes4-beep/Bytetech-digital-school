Meet Ashezi

A nine-year-old who started reading under a tree

At nine, Ashezi noticed that children living in displacement camps around her had lost their homes, and also their books and classrooms. She began gathering them under trees and in temporary shelters to read together.

That small circle has grown into Book Buddies by Ashezi Initiative, restoring learning for vulnerable children and inspiring young people across Nigeria.

“Millions of children in Africa cannot read. I was lucky to have access to books. Now I’m helping children like me discover the power of reading.”

What we do

Our core programmes

Literacy clubs in IDP camps

Safe, structured reading circles providing books and guided learning for displaced children across Abuja and Maiduguri.

Girl-child education support

Interventions that give girls in underserved communities books, mentorship and safe learning spaces.

Digital literacy hubs

Introducing children in underserved communities to digital skills and technology.

Teacher support initiative

Resources, training and sustained support for educators in fragile learning environments.

Youth ambassador programme

Empowering young leaders aged 11–24 to champion literacy and education equity.

Community and outreach

Literacy festivals, storytelling sessions and community events that celebrate reading.
