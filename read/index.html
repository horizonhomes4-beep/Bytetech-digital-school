1. Connect your own Gmail account

This page requests Google's read-only Gmail permission. You will see Google's consent screen and can cancel at any time. It retrieves the newest message's sender, subject and readable email body (up to 20,000 characters).

This demo does not ask for your Gmail password. Never enter your password into this page.

Privacy and safety

Use only your own Gmail account or an account whose owner has explicitly agreed. The app requests the Gmail readonly scope. The access token is kept in this browser tab's memory and is not sent to a separate server by this demo. Disconnecting clears the local token and displayed message. Do not use this page on a shared or public computer.

Google may show an “unverified app” warning while your own OAuth project is in testing. Only proceed if the OAuth project and client ID are yours and the consent screen clearly identifies your project.
