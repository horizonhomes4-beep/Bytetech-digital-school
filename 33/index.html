A youth-led literacy movement

Opening Books. Unlocking Futures.

Book Buddies by Ashezi Initiative is a youth-led literacy movement founded in October 2023 by a 9-year-old book lover determined to ensure that children affected by displacement and poverty do not lose access to education.

“Millions of children in Africa cannot read. I was lucky to have access to books. Now I’m helping children like me discover the power of reading—especially those displaced by conflict and climate change.”

Meet Ashezi

Our Founder’s Vision

At the age of nine, Ashezi Akwashiki noticed that many children living in displacement camps around her had lost access not only to their homes but also to books and classrooms.

She began gathering them under trees and in temporary shelters to read together, turning compassion into action.

Today, that small effort has grown into Book Buddies by Ashezi Initiative, restoring learning opportunities for vulnerable children and inspiring young people across Nigeria.
