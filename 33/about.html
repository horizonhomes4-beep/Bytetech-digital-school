Who we are

Every Child Deserves the Opportunity to Read, Learn, and Dream

Book Buddies by Ashezi Initiative is a youth-led non-profit organization dedicated to transforming lives through literacy, education, leadership, and advocacy. Founded in October 2023 by then nine-year-old author and literacy advocate Ashezi Akwashiki.

Today, Book Buddies is empowering children in underserved communities, rural schools, and Internally Displaced Persons (IDP) camps across Nigeria by creating safe and engaging spaces where reading becomes a pathway to confidence, opportunity, and hope.

Our work includes reading clubs, book drives, literacy festivals, teacher support and training, community libraries, mentorship programmes, educational outreach, digital learning initiatives, and the Book Buddies Changemakers Club.

Since our inception, we have reached over 5,000 children, distributed more than 10,000 books, raised over ₦14 million to support literacy programmes, and built partnerships with schools, volunteers, organizations, and communities.

Our Mission

To empower children and young people through literacy, education, leadership, digital inclusion, and community-driven initiatives that unlock their potential and inspire lasting social change.

Our Vision

A world where every child has equitable access to books, quality education, and opportunities to become a lifelong learner and transformational leader.
